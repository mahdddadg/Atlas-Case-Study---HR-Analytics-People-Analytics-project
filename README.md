# Atlas Labs HR Analytics — Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Human Resources Analytics dashboard built in Microsoft Power BI** for a fictional software company, Atlas Labs.

The objective of the project was to transform employee-level HR data into an interactive reporting solution that helps stakeholders understand **workforce composition, employee demographics, performance, and employee attrition**.

The analysis combines **data modeling, exploratory data analysis, DAX, data visualization, and dashboard design** to provide both high-level KPIs and detailed employee-level insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

* How large is the current workforce?
* How many employees are active versus inactive?
* How has employee hiring and attrition changed over time?
* Which departments and job roles have the largest workforce?
* What does the employee population look like by age, gender, ethnicity, and marital status?
* How are employee satisfaction and performance changing over time?
* Which factors are associated with higher employee attrition?
* How do business travel, overtime requirements, and employee tenure relate to attrition?

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

Provides a high-level view of the Atlas Labs workforce, including:

* Total Employees
* Active Employees
* Inactive Employees
* Overall Attrition Rate
* Employee hiring trends
* Active employees by department
* Active employees by department and job role

The dashboard shows a workforce of **1,470 employees**, with **1,233 active employees**, **237 inactive employees**, and an overall **16.1% attrition rate**.

### 2. Demographics

Explores the composition of the workforce through:

* Youngest and oldest employee
* Employee distribution by age group
* Gender distribution across age groups
* Marital status
* Employee ethnicity
* Average salary by ethnicity

This page helps identify the demographic characteristics of the workforce and provides a foundation for understanding workforce diversity and compensation patterns.

### 3. Performance Tracker

An interactive employee-level analysis that allows users to select an individual employee and track:

* Job Satisfaction
* Relationship Satisfaction
* Self Rating
* Environment Satisfaction
* Work-Life Balance
* Manager Rating
* Start Date
* Last Review Date
* Next Review Date

This page demonstrates how Power BI can be used not only for aggregated reporting but also for **individual employee performance monitoring**.

### 4. Attrition Analysis

The attrition page focuses on identifying potential factors associated with employee turnover.

The analysis examines attrition by:

* Department and Job Role
* Business Travel Frequency
* Overtime Requirement
* Hire Date
* Years at Company / Tenure

One of the key patterns identified is that employees with **overtime requirements and frequent business travel show substantially higher attrition rates** than employees in lower-risk categories.

## 💡 Key Takeaways

The project demonstrates how HR data can be transformed into actionable business intelligence.

Rather than focusing only on descriptive statistics, the dashboard connects workforce metrics with employee satisfaction, demographics, performance, and attrition indicators to help stakeholders identify areas that may require further investigation.

The final report was designed with a consistent **Atlas Labs brand identity**, interactive navigation, KPI cards, analytical visualizations, and employee-level filtering to create a clean and stakeholder-friendly experience.
